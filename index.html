About CiviSync

CiviSync is a state-of-the-art AI-Based digital grievance redressal portal crafted for the citizens of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

Designed to be the bridge between the public and the Municipal Corporation, this intelligent platform dynamically routes your issues—whether related to sanitation, infrastructure, water, or electricity—straight to the correct departmental authorities.

  • Real-Time Tracking: Monitor your complaint from submission to resolution.
  • Automated Workflows: Eliminates delays through smart departmental routing.
  • Multilingual Accessibility: Designed for every citizen, supporting regional languages.
